Aurora Mobility Inc. — Q3 2024 note from the CEO

From: Priya Natarajan
Date: October 21, 2024

Q3 2024 was our best quarter yet. Revenue landed at $368.5 million, up 40% year-over-year. Gross margin expanded to 50.5%. EBITDA reached $81.2 million, and GAAP net income was $49.6 million.

Public Network brought in $183.2 million, up 44% year-over-year. The biggest yield drivers were FleetOS-linked dynamic pricing and FastLane reservations, which we rolled out widely in August. Dynamic pricing alone added roughly $14 million of incremental gross profit through Q3. International revenue (outside the U.S.) rose to 31% of the total, up from 24% a year ago, led by Germany, the Netherlands, and the U.K.

Fleet Solutions grew to $126.4 million, up 56% year-over-year. We closed four deals above $8 million in total contract value — last year’s Q3 had one. The largest was a five-year, $95 million expansion with a European grocery distributor electrifying 62 depots. Net revenue retention for Fleet Solutions hit 124%, an all-time high. FleetOS went generally available two months early and was attached to 71% of new fleet contracts signed in the quarter.

Residential was $58.9 million, basically flat quarter-over-quarter, which is what we planned. We’re still leaning away from paid home-charger ads and toward OEM bundling partnerships. Member count reached 4.52 million; monthly active members were 2.9 million. ARPM improved to $31.80 — the sixth straight quarterly increase.

We finished September with 34,100 active stalls and 2,975 employees. Annualized revenue per employee is around $123,800.

Looking ahead, we expect full-year 2024 revenue of $1.38–1.42 billion, and we’re still on track for mid-teens GAAP net margin by Q4.

— Priya Natarajan, CEO
