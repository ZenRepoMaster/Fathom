Aurora Mobility Inc. — Q1 2024 note from the CEO

From: Priya Natarajan
Date: April 18, 2024

I want to be direct: Q1 2024 felt like a real shift for us. We brought in $312.8 million in revenue — up 45% from last year, and the best first quarter we’ve ever posted. Gross margin moved up to 48.5%. That came from busier public chargers and a healthier mix of Fleet Solutions deals that include our software.

Public Network did $154.6 million, up 51% year-over-year, and made up 49.4% of total revenue. We turned on 1,840 new DC fast-charge stalls, which puts us at 28,600 active stalls across North America and Western Europe. Highway corridor sites ran at 34% utilization, up from 27% a year ago.

Fleet Solutions was the real standout. Revenue hit $102.8 million — up 59% year-over-year — as depot charging and our FleetOS energy-management software crossed 420 commercial fleets. New fleet logos came in with an average contract value of $890,000 a year, 22% higher than before. Three national logistics operators moved from pilot into multi-site rollouts and added about $18 million of incremental annualized revenue.

Residential came in at $55.4 million, up 14% year-over-year. Growth slowed on purpose — we pointed marketing dollars at public and fleet work where the return is better. We shipped 61,000 HomeLink wallboxes, and the managed-charging subscription attach rate rose to 38%.

We closed March with 3.58 million registered network members. Active members were charging 4.1 times a month on average, and ARPM climbed to $29.10 from $24.40 in Q1 2023, helped by idle-fee changes and FastLane pricing.

On the bottom line: GAAP net income was $24.6 million, versus a $14.6 million loss a year earlier. EBITDA was $52.8 million. We ended the quarter with 2,710 people on staff.

Our priorities haven’t changed — denser corridors, more software on fleet deals, and deeper European coverage. Capex was $94 million, mostly stalls and grid interconnection.

— Priya Natarajan, CEO
